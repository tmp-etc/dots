# HOW
---
- Focus on **<u>[[Hack Like a Pirate - Douglas Day’s Blog.pdf|goals]]</u>**, not bugs! It's hunting after all, not just wandering aimlessly! You go in with a goal but you might find something better along the way to shift your attention to.
- Approach a topic/target with the attitude of 'fuck yeah **<u>I can exploit it</u>**'!
	- Don't discredit your ideas because it's not *canonical* (that is, not in accordance to best practicies; **<u>never assume that *'this would never happen'*</u>**, unless obvious). Let your mind flow first, review/edit later^[Don't pay much attention to the format when in the 'flow', be it mind maps, text notes etc]
- Divide, prioritize/heatmap, conquer!
- Notes help, but hacking is not just checking boxes! Think, research etc. You are a dancer of thought...
